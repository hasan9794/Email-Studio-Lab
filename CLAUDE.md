# Email Studio

This studio plans and writes email campaigns for a small business. We plan campaigns, draft the emails and keep the brand voice consistent. Nothing gets sent from here.

## Folders

- `brand/` – the brand voice guide and other brand rules.
- `leads/` – lists of people who signed up (sample or made-up data only).
- `campaigns/` – campaign plans and the email drafts for each campaign.
- `notes/` – research and decisions, such as which skills we chose and why.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Use short sentences and everyday words.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
