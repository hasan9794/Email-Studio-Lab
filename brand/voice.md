# Kettlebird Tea Brand Voice

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching The Steep Club, a monthly tea subscription box. Our first emails go to people who joined the waitlist.

We want to sound like a friend sharing a good cup of tea at home. Think of how Tapal Danedar talks: family, home and the everyday cup shared with people you love.

## 1. Our voice in three words

- **Warm.** Friendly and kind, like talking to someone at your kitchen table.
- **Curious.** We love tea and we like to share what we learn: where a tea comes from, how to brew it, what to pair it with.
- **Bright.** Cheerful and full of energy, but never loud.

## 2. Words we use

- "Taste in every sip"
- "Moments of Happiness"
- cup, sip, steep, brew
- "your Steep Club box"
- share, together, home, family
- try, discover, enjoy

Write in plain English. Use short sentences and everyday words.

## 3. Words we avoid

- "Buy Now"
- "Limited Time Only"
- Other pressure tactics: "last chance", "hurry", "don't miss out", fake countdowns
- Shouting in ALL CAPS

We invite people. We never push them.

## 4. Claims we must never make

- **No health claims of any kind.** That includes:
  - "detox"
  - "boosts your immune system"
  - "burns fat"
  - "cures stress" or any promise to heal or treat anything
- **No "doctor approved"** or any medical endorsement.

We talk about taste, comfort and the moments tea brings. Never about what it does to your body.

## 5. How we sign off

Every email ends with a note from the founder:
