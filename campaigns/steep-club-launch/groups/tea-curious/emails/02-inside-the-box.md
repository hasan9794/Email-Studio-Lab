If you only know one or two teas, this box is a gentle place to start. Here are the three teas in our first box.

**Assam black tea, from India**  
Rich and malty, with a deep amber colour. Lovely with a splash of milk.  
*Brew it:* just-boiled water, 3 to 4 minutes, your favourite mug.

**Jasmine green tea, from Fujian, China**  
Light and soft, with the scent of jasmine flowers.  
*Brew it:* water just off the boil, 2 to 3 minutes, a small cup.

**Rooibos, from South Africa**  
Sweet and warm, and an easy first tea to try if you're new to all this.  
*Brew it:* just-boiled water, 5 minutes, a big cosy mug.

Every tea comes with a brewing card like this, so you never have to guess. Just the water, the time and the cup.
