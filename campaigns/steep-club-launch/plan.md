# The Steep Club Launch: Campaign Plan

**Brand:** Kettlebird Tea
**Channel:** Email only
**Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks)
**Status:** Draft plan. Nothing is sent from this studio.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** *Your First Steep*

**Summary:** Over four weeks, we invite our 2,000 waitlist members to start The Steep Club, with emails shaped for beginners, gift buyers and daily tea drinkers.

**Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start a Steep Club subscription between 7 December 2026 and 3 January 2027. That is 15% of the list.

**Other goals:**

- Keep the list healthy. Unsubscribes stay under 1% per email.
- Learn which group joins most, so we know who to write for next.
- Get 30 or more gift subscriptions, since the campaign runs through the holidays.

**A note on dates.** The first Monday of December is the 7th. That puts Christmas in week 3 and New Year in week 4. This helps us. Gift buyers have a real reason to act, and daily drinkers can start the new year with a new cup.

---

## 2. Target audience

All 2,000 people chose to join the waitlist. They already like us. Our job is to welcome them, not to win them over from scratch.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What holds them back | Stage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** | People who enjoy tea but only know one or two kinds. | To discover new teas without feeling lost. | "I won't know how to brew it." "What if I don't like it?" | Considering |
| **Gift buyers** | People shopping for someone they love. | A thoughtful gift that feels personal and arrives on time. | "Will it arrive before Christmas?" "Can I choose how many months?" | Ready to decide |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | People who have tea every day, often more than once. | Variety in their daily cup, and good tea they don't have to hunt for. | "Is this better than what I already buy?" "Can I skip a month?" | Ready to decide |

**Where they spend time:** their inbox. This plan is email only, so every message must earn its place.

**If we don't know someone's group:** they get the general version of each email. The first email asks them to tell us their group with one click.

---

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:**
*Each month, your Steep Club box brings new teas to discover and share at home, with taste in every sip.*

**Supporting messages:**

1. **Discover something new each month.** Every box has teas from different places, with notes on where each one comes from.
   *Proof:* a sample box list with tea names and origins.
2. **We show you how to brew it.** Each tea comes with a simple brewing card: water, time, cup.
   *Proof:* a photo of a real brewing card.
3. **A gift that keeps arriving.** One gift means a new box every month. Each one is a small moment of happiness.
   *Proof:* a gift message card and clear delivery dates.
4. **It fits your life.** Skip a month or stop any time.
   *Proof:* the plain-English subscription terms.

**Message for each group:**

| Group | Lead with | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Beginners | Discovery and simple brewing | "Not sure where to start? We'll steep it with you." |
| Gift buyers | Thoughtful gift, clear delivery dates | "A cup to share, every month of the year." |
| Daily drinkers | Variety and quality in the daily cup | "Your everyday cup, with something new to try." |

**Voice checks for every email:**

- Warm, curious and bright. Short sentences, everyday words.
- We invite. We never push. No "Buy Now", "Limited Time Only", "last chance", "hurry", "don't miss out" or countdowns.
- No ALL CAPS.
- **No health claims at all.** We talk about taste, comfort and moments. Never what tea does to your body.
- Every email ends with:
  > Warmly,
  > Hasan
  > Founder, Kettlebird Tea

**Calls to action we use:** "Start your Steep Club", "Send a box to someone you love", "Take a look inside the box", "Try your first box".

## 4. Channel strategy

**Email (the only channel)**

- **Why it fits:** Everyone in the audience gave us their email and asked to hear from us. Email is cheap, easy to track, and lets us send a different version to each group.
- **Format:** Short, simple emails. One main idea and one button each. A photo of the box or a cup of tea where it helps.
- **Effort:** Medium. Seven emails, most with three group versions.
- **Budget:** Almost all spend goes to the email tool, a few product photos and the cost of any welcome gift (see section 8).

**What we use that we already own:**

- **The Steep Club sign-up page** on our website. Every email links here. It must be live before 7 December.
- **Our email tool** for sending, splitting the list into groups and tracking results.

**What we leave out, on purpose:** paid ads, influencers and press. That fits the small budget. We can look at them after launch if the list responds well.

## 7. Success metrics

**Main measure:** subscriptions started by waitlist members. **Target: 300 by Sunday 3 January 2027.**

**Weekly targets to stay on track:**

| By the end of | Total joined | Why this shape |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 (13 Dec) | 110 | Launch week gets the most excited people. |
| Week 2 (20 Dec) | 200 | The gift push before Christmas. |
| Week 3 (27 Dec) | 240 | Holiday week is usually quiet. |
| Week 4 (3 Jan) | 300 | New-year interest and the founder's note. |

**Other measures:**

| Measure | Target | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 45% or more | Email tool |
| Click rate | 8% or more | Email tool |
| Click to join rate | 20% or more | Sign-up page with tracking links in each email |
| Joins by group | Watch, no target | Group tag in the email tool |
| Gift subscriptions | 30 or more | Gift option on the sign-up page |
| Unsubscribes | Under 1% per email | Email tool |

**When we report:** a short check every Monday and Thursday after each send, and a full review in the week of 4 January 2027.

**A note on the target.** 15% of a list joining in one month is high. Warm waitlists often do well, but this goal is a stretch. If we are under 100 by the end of week 1, we should look again at the offer (see section 9).

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What we'll do |
|---|---|
| **Fewer people join than we hope.** 300 is a stretch. | Check numbers every Monday. If we're under 100 after week 1, test a new subject line, make the welcome gift clearer, or add a "try one box" option. No pressure words. |
| **Gift boxes don't arrive by Christmas.** This would hurt trust. | Confirm the real delivery cut-off with our shipping partner before Email 4. Only print a date we are sure of. After that date, offer "gift it for the new year" instead. |
| **Too many emails during a busy season.** People may unsubscribe. | Stop sending launch emails to anyone who joins. Skip Christmas Eve. If unsubscribes go over 1% on any email, cut Email 6. |
| **A message slips off-brand.** For example, a health claim or a pushy line. | Run every email through a brand voice check before it's final. Hasan approves each one. |
